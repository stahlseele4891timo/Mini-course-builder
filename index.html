Team activity

Brand an unfamiliar airport

Work in a team to build a marketing and branding plan for an airport you know nothing about, then pitch it to your peers.

1

Your brief

Create a marketing and branding plan for an airport located in one of these countries:

  • Team 1 Albania
  • Team 2 Papua New Guinea
  • Team 3 Azerbaijan
  • Team 4 Eritrea
  • Team 5 Paraguay

Why these countries?

You hold no existing brand associations for them. You must build salience, meaning, response and resonance from nothing.

2

Required deliverables (Google Slides)

  1. Positioning statement

    Your target segment, and what the brand promises them (Step 1 of the process).

  2. Brand elements

    Use as many as you sensibly can: name, logo concept, slogan, character or mascot, URL, jingle idea, signature packaging or visual identity.

  3. Resonance plan

    One concrete initiative per pyramid level: how you build salience, meaning, response and resonance.

  4. Marketing channels

    Three channels with reasons (B2C), plus one B2B action toward airlines (Lesson 16).

  5. Secondary brand association

    One you will leverage: country, event, co-brand, licensing and so on.

3

Pitches and judging (30 min)

4-minute pitch per team: sell us the airport.

Peer judging (1–5 each)

Coherence

Do all elements tell ONE story?

Framework fidelity

Is every pyramid level genuinely addressed?

Feasibility

Could this airport actually execute this plan?

Debrief

Which element carried each brand? What did teams reach for first, and does that match how real brands are built?
